title:
  en: "More Reliable Muting and Media Pausing"
  zh: "静音与媒体暂停能力增强"
content:
  en: |
    - Refactored Mute to use the browser's native tab-muting API, providing more complete and reliable audio control while preserving mute states managed by the user, Hide, other extensions, or tab capture.
    - Enhanced Pause media with persistent support for accessible iframes, open Shadow DOM, and dynamically added audio and video elements. Turning the feature off never resumes media automatically.
  zh: |
    - 重构 Mute 静音机制，改用浏览器原生标签页静音 API，在提供更完整、可靠声音控制的同时，妥善保留由用户、Hide、其他扩展或标签页捕获管理的静音状态。
    - 增强 Pause media 媒体暂停能力，持续支持可访问的 iframe、开放式 Shadow DOM，以及动态加载的音频和视频元素；关闭功能时不会自动恢复播放。
