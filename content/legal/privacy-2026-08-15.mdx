  en: |
    ## 1. Data Collection

    This browser extension does not collect, store, or transmit any personal private data of users, including but not limited to passwords, browsing history, payment information, and personal identity information.

    ## 2. Data Storage

    All configurations and authorization status of the extension are stored only on the user's local device and will not be uploaded to any external servers.

    ## 3. Third-Party Sharing

    We will not share or sell user data with any third parties, and will only provide necessary information to regulatory authorities in compliance with legal requirements.

    ## 4. Payment Data

    Payment-related data is independently processed by the Paddle platform in accordance with its privacy policy. This tool does not obtain any payment-related information.

    ## 5. Permissions and Local Processing

    The extension only requests browser permissions (such as tab management and storage) that are necessary to provide keyword matching, muting, hiding, and cleanup features. Keyword matching and the comparison of tab URLs and titles all happen locally in your browser; this data never leaves your device.
  zh: |
    ## 1. 数据收集

    本浏览器插件不会收集、存储、传输用户的任何个人隐私数据，包括但不限于密码、浏览历史、支付信息、个人身份信息。

    ## 2. 数据存储

    插件的所有配置、授权状态仅存储在用户本地设备，不会上传至任何外部服务器。

    ## 3. 第三方共享

    我们不会向任何第三方共享、出售用户数据，仅在法律要求的情况下配合监管机构提供必要信息。

    ## 4. 支付数据

    支付相关数据由 Paddle 平台独立处理，遵循其隐私政策，本工具不获取任何支付相关信息。

    ## 5. 权限与本地数据处理

    本插件仅在必要时申请浏览器权限（如标签页管理、存储），用于实现关键词匹配、静音、隐藏及清理等功能。关键词匹配、标签页 URL 与标题的比对等处理均在本机浏览器内完成，相关数据不会离开您的设备。
